About the project

A learner's notebook, open to the world.

DecodeTheWorld is a personal portfolio and learning space for ideas about education, technology, self-improvement, and the skills that help people navigate life with more clarity.

This is a work in progress. The goal is not to pretend to know everything — it is to learn carefully, explain what is useful, and improve with every project.
